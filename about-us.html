About Bebehug

Bebehug is the online store for Dr.Bohrras Baby Care, operated by Boharras Inc. in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. The store presents selected reusable baby-care sheets and bed-protection products for customers shopping online in India.

Our current product range includes Feel Dry washable quick-drying sheets, Double Colour Sterilizable Rubber Sheets, and Magic Mat printed rubber sheets. Product pages provide available sizes, colour options, descriptions and prices so customers can review the options before placing an order.

How we serve customers

  • We display product options and prices on the website before checkout.
  • Customers can submit an order inquiry through WhatsApp or use the online checkout where payment is enabled.
  • We provide order and delivery assistance through our customer-support contact channel.
