How to Choose a Baby Rubber Sheet or Mattress Protector

When comparing baby rubber sheets and washable mattress protectors, start with the surface you need to protect, the size of the area, and how you want to clean and reuse the product. These products can look similar but their top surfaces and intended uses may differ.

1. Understand the product types

Feel Dry washable quick-drying sheet

Feel Dry has a soft polar-fleece top with wicking treatment bonded to a TPU waterproof backing. The top is designed to help spread liquid across the surface while the backing helps protect the mattress from moisture. Choose it when you want a washable sheet with a soft top surface and waterproof backing.

Sizes: S — 70 × 50 cm; M — 70 × 100 cm; L — 140 × 100 cm; XL — 140 × 200 cm. Available colours are Pink, Blue, Maroon, Pista, Red and Rose.

Double Colour Sterilizable Rubber Sheet

This reusable sheet has a pink-and-blue double-colour design. Compare the dimensions with the area you want to cover before ordering, and follow the product's own care instructions for cleaning and sterilization.

Sizes: 90 × 100 cm — ₹400; 90 × 200 cm — ₹700; 90 × 300 cm — ₹900.

Magic Mat printed rubber sheet

Magic Mat is a reusable printed sheet available in pink-printed and blue-printed options. Check whether the smaller square size or the longer rectangular size better suits the surface you are covering.

Sizes: 90 × 90 cm — ₹300; 90 × 180 cm — ₹500.

2. Measure the area before choosing a size

  1. Measure the length and width of the mattress, cot, changing area or other intended surface.
  2. Compare your measurements with the product dimensions listed on the product page. Do not assume products with similar names have the same dimensions.
  3. Think about whether you need to cover only one section or a larger area. Select the product based on its intended use and fit, not price alone.
  4. Confirm the selected size, colour, quantity and current price before adding the item to your cart.

3. Check the top surface and backing

A washable mattress protector with a textile top and waterproof backing behaves differently from a rubber sheet with a smooth surface. If you want a soft, wicking top, review the Feel Dry details. If you want the double-colour or printed sheet design, compare the Double Colour Rubber Sheet and Magic Mat pages.

4. Read the care instructions

  • Follow the care and cleaning instructions supplied with the specific product.
  • Do not assume that every product can be washed, boiled, ironed or sterilized in the same way.
  • Allow a washed product to dry as directed before storing it.
  • Inspect the sheet periodically and stop using it if it becomes damaged or no longer performs as intended.

For infant sleep: Follow current safe-sleep guidance and the product's intended-use instructions. Do not add loose or unapproved bedding layers to an infant sleep space.

Frequently asked questions

Are a baby rubber sheet and a washable mattress protector the same?

Not always. Surface material and construction can differ. Review each product's description to check whether it has a textile top, wicking treatment, waterproof backing or a smooth rubber-sheet surface.

Which size should I buy?

Measure the intended area and match it with the product dimensions listed above. If you are unsure which size fits, contact customer support before ordering.

Can I use the same cleaning method for all three products?

No single cleaning method should be assumed for all products. Follow the care instructions for the specific item you purchase.
